P: One hot afternoon, a big lion was sleeping under a tree in the forest. A little mouse came out of its hole to look for food. It did not see the lion, and it ran right across his nose.
P: The lion woke up with a loud roar. He caught the mouse under his huge paw. "How dare you wake me up?" he shouted. "Now I am going to eat you!"
P: The mouse was shaking with fear. "Please let me go," it cried. "I am too small to be a good meal for you. If you set me free, one day I will help you."
P: The lion laughed. "How can a tiny mouse like you ever help a great lion like me?" But he thought the mouse was funny, and he was not very hungry. So he lifted his paw and let the mouse go.
P: A few days later, some hunters came into the forest. They wanted to catch the lion and take him to the king. They put a strong net on the ground and hid it under some leaves. When the lion walked by, he stepped into the trap. The net closed around him. He pulled and bit and rolled, but he could not get out. He roared angrily, and the sound filled the whole forest.
P: The little mouse heard the roar and knew it was the lion. It ran as fast as it could. When it found him, it began to bite through the ropes of the net with its sharp little teeth. Soon there was a big hole in the net, and the lion walked out, free.
P: "You laughed at me," said the mouse, "but now you know that even a little mouse can help a lion."
P: The lesson of the story is simple: no act of kindness is ever wasted.

title: Why Do Leaves Change Color?
zh: 树叶为什么会变色？
level: B1
genre: 科学
src: facts
sum: 叶绿素让树叶呈绿色；秋天树停止制造叶绿素，原本藏着的黄色、橙色就显露出来，有的树还会产生新的红色物质。最后树叶脱落，帮助树过冬。
P: In many parts of the world, autumn is a colorful season. Leaves that were green all summer turn yellow, orange and red. Then they fall to the ground. Why does this happen?
P: The answer starts with a green substance called chlorophyll. Leaves use chlorophyll to make food for the tree. With the help of sunlight, chlorophyll turns water and carbon dioxide, a gas in the air, into sugar. Because there is so much chlorophyll in leaves in spring and summer, its green color covers up all the other colors.
P: But leaves also contain yellow and orange substances. They are there all year, hidden under the green. Carrots get their orange color from one of these substances, too.
P: When autumn comes, the days get shorter and the nights get cooler. In winter there is not enough sunlight or water for leaves to make much food, so trees get ready to rest. They stop making chlorophyll. As the green slowly disappears, the yellow and orange colors finally show.
P: What about red? Red is a little different. Some trees, like many maples, make new red substances in autumn. Sunny days and cool (but not freezing) nights help them produce bright red leaves. That is why some autumns are more colorful than others.
P: Finally, a thin layer of cells grows where each leaf joins its branch. This layer slowly cuts the leaf off from the tree, and the leaf falls. Dropping its leaves helps the tree save water during the cold, dry winter.
P: Next time you see a red maple leaf, remember: you are watching science in action.

zh: 蜜蜂：小身体，大作用
level: B1
genre: 自然

P: A honeybee is only a little more than one centimeter long, but it is one of the most important insects on Earth.
P: Honeybees live together in large groups called colonies. A strong colony can have tens of thousands of bees. Each colony has one queen. Her job is to lay eggs, and in summer she can lay more than a thousand eggs a day. Most of the other bees are female worker bees. They clean the hive, feed the young bees, make wax and collect food. There are also hundreds of male bees, called drones.
P: Worker bees fly from flower to flower to collect nectar, a sweet liquid, and pollen, a yellow powder. Back at the hive, they turn the nectar into honey and store it for the winter. To make just one jar of honey, bees must visit about two million flowers.
P: How do bees tell each other where the flowers are? They dance! When a worker finds good flowers, she returns to the hive and does a special "waggle dance." The direction of the dance tells the other bees which way to fly, and the length of the dance tells them how far to go.
P: While bees collect food, they also do something very useful for us. Pollen sticks to their hairy bodies and is carried from one flower to another. This helps plants make fruit and seeds. Many of the fruits and vegetables we eat, such as apples, strawberries and pumpkins, need help from bees and other insects.
P: Sadly, in some places bee numbers are going down because of disease, the loss of wild flowers and some chemicals used on farms. People can help by planting flowers and not using harmful chemicals in their gardens. Protecting bees means protecting our food.

title: The Water Cycle
zh: 水的循环
level: B1
genre: 科学
src: facts
sum: 水在地球上不停地循环：蒸发、凝结成云、降水，再流回大海或渗入地下。能直接使用的淡水很少，所以要节约用水。
P: The water you drink today may be millions of years old. A dinosaur might once have drunk it! This is possible because Earth's water moves in a never-ending circle called the water cycle.
P: The cycle has no real beginning or end, but let's start with the ocean. When the sun heats the water in oceans, lakes and rivers, some of it turns into an invisible gas called water vapor. This is called evaporation. Plants also give off water vapor through their leaves. The warm, wet air rises into the sky.
P: High above the ground, the air is much colder. There, the water vapor cools down and turns back into tiny drops of water. This is called condensation. Billions of these tiny drops come together to form clouds. You can see condensation at home, too: think of the drops of water on the outside of a cold glass of juice on a hot day.
P: As more and more drops join together, they become heavy. When they are too heavy to stay in the air, they fall to the ground as rain. If the air is very cold, they fall as snow or hail.
P: Some of the water runs into rivers and flows back to the sea. Some soaks into the ground and becomes groundwater, which can stay underground for a very long time. Some is frozen in ice and snow on high mountains. Sooner or later, the sun warms it again, and the cycle continues.
P: Only a very small part of Earth's water is fresh water that people can easily use. That is why it is important not to waste it. Turning off the tap while you brush your teeth is a small step, but it helps.

title: "Octopuses: Clever Animals of the Sea"
zh: 章鱼：海洋里的聪明家伙
level: B1
genre: 自然
src: facts
sum: 章鱼有八条腕、三颗心、没有骨头，大部分神经细胞长在腕上。它们会开罐子、会逃跑、会伪装，还会喷墨汁。研究章鱼让我们明白"聪明"不止一种。
P: With eight arms, three hearts and no bones, the octopus looks like something from another planet. But this strange animal is one of the smartest creatures in the ocean.
P: An octopus has about 500 million nerve cells, far more than most animals without a backbone. Surprisingly, most of these cells are not in its head but in its arms. This means each arm can touch, taste and even "think" a little on its own. The suckers on the arms can taste whatever they touch.
P: Octopuses are excellent problem solvers. In aquariums, scientists have watched them unscrew jars to get the food inside. In 2016, news reports around the world told the story of Inky, an octopus who escaped from an aquarium in New Zealand. He squeezed through a small gap in his tank, crossed the floor and slid down a pipe into the sea. In the wild, one kind of octopus collects coconut shells and carries them around as a portable house.
P: Octopuses are also masters of disguise. Special cells in their skin let them change color in less than a second. They can even change the texture of their skin to look like rocks, sand or seaweed. If a hungry shark does find one, the octopus has another trick: it shoots out a cloud of dark ink and quickly swims away.
P: Strangely, these clever animals have short lives. Most kinds live only one or two years. A mother octopus guards her eggs for weeks or even months without eating.
P: Scientists are still learning about how octopuses think. Because their brains are so different from ours, studying them helps us understand that there is more than one way to be smart.

zh: 玛丽·居里
level: B1
