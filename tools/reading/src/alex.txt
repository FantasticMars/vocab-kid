id: a01
title: My Cat Mimi
zh: 我的猫咪米米
level: 1
genre: 宠物
src: original
sum: 小作者介绍自己的白色小猫米米：爱晒太阳、早上要吃鱼、追红色小球，晚上趴在腿上听故事。
P: I have a cat. Her name is Mimi. She is small and white. She has big green eyes and a long tail.
P: Mimi likes to sleep in the sun. She sleeps on my bed, too. In the morning, she says, "Meow!" She wants her breakfast. I give her fish and water.
P: After school, I play with Mimi. She runs after a little red ball. She is very fast!
P: At night, Mimi sits on my lap. I read a book, and she listens. I love my cat, and she loves me.
G: Mimi = 米米（猫的名字）
PH: runs after = 追赶
PH: At night = 在晚上
Q: detail | What color is Mimi? | 米米是什么颜色的？
* White.
- Black.
- Brown.
E: 第一段：She is small and white.（她又小又白。）
Q: tf | Mimi likes to sleep in the sun. | 米米喜欢在阳光下睡觉。
A: T
E: 第二段：Mimi likes to sleep in the sun.
Q: detail | What does Mimi eat in the morning? | 米米早上吃什么？
* Fish.
- Rice.
- Cake.
E: 第二段：I give her fish and water.（我给她鱼和水。）
Q: detail | What does Mimi run after? | 米米追什么？
* A little red ball.
- A big dog.
- A green bird.
E: 第三段：She runs after a little red ball.

PH: go back to = 回到

Q: tf | The classroom is on the first floor. | 教室在一楼。

id: a05
title: A Rainy Day
zh: 下雨天
level: 2
genre: 天气
src: original
sum: 星期六下雨不能去公园，小作者很难过。爸爸想了个好主意：穿上雨衣雨靴去踩水坑！下雨天也可以很好玩。
P: Today is Saturday. I want to go to the park, but it is raining. The sky is gray. I can hear the rain on the window: pitter-patter, pitter-patter.
P: I am sad. Then Dad has an idea. "Let's put on our raincoats and boots!" he says.
P: We go outside. I jump in the puddles. Splash! Dad jumps, too. We laugh and laugh. We see a little frog. It is happy in the rain, too.
P: When we get home, we are wet. Mom gives us hot chocolate. A rainy day can be a fun day!
G: pitter-patter = 滴答滴答（雨声）
G: Dad = 爸爸
G: splash = 啪嗒（溅水声）
PH: put on = 穿上
PH: has an idea = 有了一个主意
PH: get home = 到家
PH: hot chocolate = 热巧克力（饮料）
Q: detail | What day is it today? | 今天星期几？
* Saturday.
- Sunday.
- Monday.
E: 第一段：Today is Saturday.
Q: detail | Why is the writer sad at first? | 小作者一开始为什么难过？
* Because it is raining.
- Because Dad is busy.
- Because the writer is sick.
E: 第一段：I want to go to the park, but it is raining.
Q: detail | What animal do they see? | 他们看到了什么动物？
* A frog.
- A duck.
- A cat.
E: 第三段：We see a little frog.
Q: tf | Mom gives them cold juice. | 妈妈给他们喝冷果汁。
A: F
E: 第四段：Mom gives us hot chocolate.（是热巧克力。）

title: The Little Red Hen
zh: 小红母鸡
level: 2
genre: 童话
src: folk
sum: 改编自英美传统童话。小红母鸡请猫、狗、鸭子帮忙种麦子、做面包，大家都说"我不"；面包烤好了，大家都想吃，母鸡却只和小鸡们分享。
P: A little red hen lives on a farm with a cat, a dog and a duck. One day, she finds some wheat seeds.
P: "Who will help me plant the seeds?" she asks. "Not I," says the cat. "Not I," says the dog. "Not I," says the duck. So she plants them all by herself.
P: The wheat grows tall. "Who will help me make bread?" she asks. "Not I," they all say. So she makes the bread by herself.
P: The bread smells good. "Who will help me eat the bread?" asks the hen. "I will!" say the cat, the dog and the duck. "No," says the hen. "I will eat it with my chicks."
G: smells = 闻起来
G: Not = 不
G: I = 我
PH: Not I = 我不（我才不帮）
PH: by herself = 她自己（独自）
PH: smells good = 闻起来很香
PH: One day = 有一天
Q: detail | What does the hen find? | 母鸡找到了什么？
* Some wheat seeds.
- An egg.
- A cake.
E: 第一段：she finds some wheat seeds（麦子的种子）。
Q: who | Who helps the hen plant the seeds? | 谁帮母鸡种种子？
* Nobody. She does it by herself.
- The cat.
- The duck.
E: 第二段：So she plants them all by herself.（她自己种。）
Q: tf | The dog helps the hen make bread. | 狗帮母鸡做面包。
A: F
E: 第三段："Not I," they all say. 大家都不帮忙。
Q: who | Who eats the bread at the end? | 最后谁吃了面包？
* The hen and her chicks.
- The cat and the dog.
- The duck.
E: 第四段：I will eat it with my chicks.（我和我的小鸡们一起吃。）

zh: 去动物园
level: 2
genre: 动物
