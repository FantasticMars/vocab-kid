D: 找东西 | S=Shop assistant/店员, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Excuse me, where can I find the milk? | 打扰一下，牛奶在哪里？
S: It's in the dairy section, aisle six, next to the cheese. | 在乳制品区，六号通道，奶酪旁边。
E: Thanks. And which aisle is the toothpaste in? | 谢谢。牙膏在哪个通道？
S: Toiletries are in aisle ten, at the back of the shop. | 洗漱用品在十号通道，商店后面。
E: Great. Do you sell phone chargers too? | 太好了。你们也卖手机充电器吗？
S: Yes, but only a few. They're near the checkouts. | 卖，不过不多，在收银台附近。
E: Perfect. One more thing: do you have a basket? | 太好了。还有一件事：有购物篮吗？
S: Yes, the baskets are by the entrance. | 有，购物篮在入口旁边。
E: Thank you for your help. | 谢谢你的帮助。
S: No problem. Enjoy your shopping! | 不客气。购物愉快！
D: 缺货了 | S=Shop assistant/店员, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Excuse me, I'm looking for this cereal, but the shelf is empty. | 打扰一下，我在找这种麦片，但货架空了。
S: Let me check. I'm afraid it's out of stock today. | 我查一下。恐怕今天缺货了。
E: Oh no. When will you have more? | 哦不。什么时候会再有货？
S: We get a delivery every Tuesday, so probably tomorrow. | 我们每周二进货，所以应该明天有。
E: OK. Is there anything similar? | 好的。有类似的吗？
S: This brand is very similar, and it's on offer this week. | 这个牌子很像，而且本周在打折。
E: Is it sweet? I don't like cereal with too much sugar. | 甜吗？我不喜欢太甜的麦片。
S: It's less sweet than the other one. Look, it says "low sugar". | 比那个少甜一些。你看，上面写着"低糖"。
E: Great, I'll try it. Thanks! | 太好了，我试试。谢谢！

D: 比价格 | K=Kate/凯特（寄宿家庭姐姐）, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Kate, which one is better value, the big pack or the small pack? | 凯特，哪个更划算，大包还是小包？
K: Let's compare. The big one is three pounds for one kilo. | 我们比较一下。大包一公斤三英镑。
E: And the small one is two pounds for five hundred grams. | 小包五百克两英镑。
K: So the big pack is better value. | 所以大包更划算。
E: But look, this yellow label says "buy one get one free" on the small pack. | 但是你看，小包上的黄色标签写着"买一送一"。
K: Oh, you're right! Then two small packs cost two pounds. That's a bargain! | 哦，你说得对！那两小包才两英镑，太便宜了！
E: Great. What does "best before" mean on the pack? | 太好了。包装上的"best before"是什么意思？
K: It means it's best to eat it before that date. | 意思是最好在那个日期之前吃。
E: It says the fifth of next month, so that's fine. | 上面写着下个月五号，那没问题。
K: Perfect. Let's take them. | 太好了，我们拿这个。
D: 称水果 | S=Shop assistant/店员, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Excuse me, how much are these apples? | 打扰一下，这些苹果多少钱？
S: They're two pounds per kilo. | 每公斤两英镑。
E: Do I weigh them myself? | 要我自己称吗？
S: Yes, put them on the scale over there and press the apple button. | 是的，放到那边的秤上，按苹果的按钮。
E: OK. It printed a price tag. Do I stick it on the bag? | 好的。打出了一张价签。我把它贴在袋子上吗？
S: That's right. | 对。
E: Are these organic? | 这些是有机的吗？
S: No, the organic ones are on the left. They cost a bit more. | 不是，有机的在左边，贵一点。
E: I'll take these, then. Thanks! | 那我就要这些吧。谢谢！
S: You're welcome. | 不客气。

P: Here's my receipt. | 这是我的收据。 | 店员问你要购物凭证
D: 自助结账 | S=Staff/工作人员, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Excuse me, could you help me? The machine won't scan this. | 打扰一下，你能帮帮我吗？机器扫不上这个。
S: Sure. Let me try. ... The barcode is a bit wet. There, it's done. | 好的，我试试。……条形码有点湿。好了，扫上了。
E: Thanks. Now it says "unexpected item in the bagging area". | 谢谢。现在显示"装袋区有意外物品"。
S: That's because your own bag is on the scale. I'll fix it. | 那是因为你自己的袋子放在秤上了。我来处理。
E: Sorry, I didn't know. | 抱歉，我不知道。
S: No problem. It happens a lot. Now you can pay. | 没关系，经常这样。现在可以付款了。
E: Can I pay by card here? | 这里可以刷卡吗？
S: Yes. Just tap your card on the card machine. | 可以。把卡在刷卡机上碰一下就行。
E: Done! Do I need the receipt? | 好了！我需要收据吗？
S: It's a good idea to keep it, in case you need to return something. | 最好留着，万一要退东西。
E: OK. Thanks for your help! | 好的。谢谢你的帮助！
D: 退货 | C=Customer service/客服, E=Emma/艾玛
C: Hello. How can I help you? | 你好。有什么可以帮你的？
E: Hi. I'd like to return these headphones. They're faulty. | 你好。我想退这副耳机，它有质量问题。
C: I'm sorry about that. What's wrong with them? | 很抱歉。有什么问题？
E: The left side doesn't work. | 左边没有声音。
C: Do you have the receipt? | 你有收据吗？
E: Yes, here it is. I bought them last Saturday. | 有，在这儿。我上周六买的。
C: Would you like an exchange or a refund? | 你想换货还是退款？
E: Can I get a refund, please? | 请问我可以退款吗？
C: Sure. The money will go back to your card in three to five days. | 可以。钱会在三到五天内退回你的卡上。
E: Great. Thank you! | 太好了。谢谢！
