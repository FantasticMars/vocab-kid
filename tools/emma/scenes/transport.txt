P: How much is the fare? | 车费多少钱？ | 你想知道坐车要花多少钱

D: 坐公交车 | B=Bus driver/公交司机, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Excuse me, does this bus go to the library? | 打扰一下，这趟车去图书馆吗？
B: Yes, it does. Hop on! | 去的，上来吧！
E: How much is the fare? | 车费多少钱？
B: It's three dollars. You can tap your transit card here. | 三美元。你可以在这里刷交通卡。
E: I don't have a transit card yet. Can I pay with cash? | 我还没有交通卡。我可以付现金吗？
B: Sure, but I need exact change. Put the coins in this box. | 可以，但要正好的零钱。把硬币投进这个箱子。
E: Here you go. Could you tell me when to get off? | 给您。到站时能告诉我一声吗？
B: No problem. The library is the fifth stop. | 没问题。图书馆是第五站。
E: How will I know it's my stop? | 我怎么知道到站了？
B: Just press the button when you see the library. Then the bus will stop. | 看到图书馆时按一下按钮，车就会停。
E: Thank you so much! | 太谢谢您了！
D: 坐地铁 | S=Station staff/地铁站工作人员, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Hi, how do I buy a ticket for the subway? | 你好，地铁票怎么买？
S: You can use the ticket machine over there. | 你可以用那边的自动售票机。
E: Should I get a one-way ticket or a day pass? | 我应该买单程票还是一日通票？
S: If you're taking more than two trips today, the day pass is cheaper. | 如果你今天坐两次以上，一日通票更便宜。
E: Then I'd like a day pass, please. I'm going to the zoo. | 那我想买一日通票。我要去动物园。
S: Take the red line and transfer to the green line at Union Station. | 坐红线，在联合站换乘绿线。
E: Which platform is the train to the zoo? | 去动物园的车在哪个站台？
S: Platform two, down the stairs. Mind the gap when you get on the train. | 二号站台，下楼梯就是。上车时小心站台间隙。
E: How long does it take to get there? | 到那里要多长时间？
S: About thirty minutes. This route is quick. | 大约三十分钟。这条路线很快。
E: Great. Is the zoo far from downtown? | 太好了。动物园离市中心远吗？
S: Not really. Enjoy your day! | 不太远。祝你玩得开心！

P: Excuse me, how do I get to the science museum? | 打扰一下，去科学博物馆怎么走？ | 你找不到科学博物馆
P: Is it far from here? | 离这里远吗？ | 你想知道要走多远
P: Go straight for two blocks and turn right. | 直走两个街区然后右转。 | 有人问你路，你来指路
P: It's across from the park. | 它在公园对面。 | 你在描述一个地方的位置
P: I think we're lost. | 我觉得我们迷路了。 | 你和朋友找不到路了
P: Can you show me on the map? | 你能在地图上指给我看吗？ | 你听不太懂路线，想看地图
P: Wait for the walk signal before you cross the street. | 等行人信号灯亮了再过马路。 | 你提醒朋友注意安全
P: It's just around the corner. | 拐个弯就到了。 | 目的地就在附近
D: 向路人问路 | M=Man/路人, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Excuse me, how do I get to the science museum? | 打扰一下，去科学博物馆怎么走？
M: Sure. Go straight for two blocks and turn right at the traffic lights. | 好的。直走两个街区，在红绿灯处右转。
E: Two blocks, then turn right at the traffic lights? | 两个街区，然后在红绿灯处右转？
M: That's right. Then the museum is on your left. | 对。然后博物馆就在你的左边。
E: Is it far from here? | 离这里远吗？
M: No, it's about a ten-minute walk. | 不远，走路大约十分钟。
E: What does the building look like? | 那栋楼是什么样子的？
M: It's a big white building across from the park. You can't miss it. | 是一栋白色的大楼，在公园对面。你一定能看到。
E: Thank you very much! | 非常感谢您！
M: You're welcome. Have fun at the museum! | 不客气。在博物馆玩得开心！
D: 看地图找路 | J=Jack/杰克, E=Emma/艾玛
J: Emma, I think we're lost. Where is the ice cream shop? | 艾玛，我觉得我们迷路了。冰淇淋店在哪儿？
E: Don't worry. Let me check the map on my phone. | 别担心。我看看手机上的地图。
J: Should we go back the way we came? | 我们要不要按原路返回？
E: No, we just need to cross the street and walk to the corner. | 不用，我们只要过马路，走到拐角就行。
J: OK. Let's use the crosswalk. | 好。我们走人行横道吧。
E: Wait for the walk signal before you cross the street. | 等行人信号灯亮了再过马路。
J: It's green now. Let's go. | 现在绿灯了。走吧。
E: Look, the shop is next to the gas station on Main Avenue. | 看，那家店就在主街加油站旁边。
J: I see it! It's just around the corner. | 我看到了！拐个弯就到了。
E: Great. Next time, let's look at the map first. | 太好了。下次我们先看地图吧。

D: 第一次坐校车 | J=Mr. Jones/琼斯先生（校车司机）, E=Emma/艾玛
J: Good morning! You must be the new student. | 早上好！你一定是新同学吧。
E: Yes, I'm Emma. Is this the school bus to Maple Middle School? | 是的，我叫艾玛。这是去枫叶中学的校车吗？
J: It sure is. Find a free seat and stay in your seat while the bus is moving. | 没错。找个空座位，车开的时候请坐着别动。
E: OK. What time does the school bus pick us up every day? | 好的。校车每天几点来接我们？
J: I pick you up at this bus stop at 7:40. Please be on time. | 我每天七点四十在这个车站接你。请准时。
E: And where does the bus drop us off after school? | 放学后校车在哪里放我们下车？
J: Right here, at the same stop, around 3:30. | 就在这里，同一个车站，三点半左右。
E: What should I do if I miss the bus? | 如果我错过了校车该怎么办？
J: Then ask your host family to give you a ride to school. | 那就请你的寄宿家庭开车送你去学校。
E: Got it. Thank you, Mr. Jones. | 明白了。谢谢您，琼斯先生。
D: 骑车去同学家 | V=Dave/戴夫（寄宿爸爸）, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Dave, can I ride my bike to Mia's house after school? | 戴夫，放学后我可以骑车去米娅家吗？
V: Sure. How far is it? | 可以。有多远？
E: It's about ten minutes. There's a bike lane on the way. | 大约十分钟。路上有自行车道。
V: Good. Don't forget to wear your helmet. | 好。别忘了戴头盔。
E: I won't. Where is my bike lock? | 不会忘的。我的自行车锁在哪儿？
V: It's in the garage. Lock your bike when you get there. | 在车库里。到了以后把车锁好。
E: Oh no, my bike has a flat tire! | 哦不，我的自行车轮胎瘪了！
V: Don't worry. I can fix it in five minutes. | 别担心，我五分钟就能修好。
E: Thanks! I'll be back by five. | 谢谢！我五点前回来。
V: OK. Ride safely and have fun! | 好的。骑车注意安全，玩得开心！
