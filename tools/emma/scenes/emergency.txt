D: 冷静拨打911 | O=Operator/911接线员, E=Emma/艾玛
O: 911, what's your emergency? | 这里是911，请问有什么紧急情况？
E: Hi. An old man fell down in the park. He can't get up. Please send an ambulance. | 你好。公园里有位老爷爷摔倒了，站不起来。请派一辆救护车来。
O: OK. You're doing great. What's the address? | 好的，你做得很好。地址是哪里？
E: It's Maple Park, next to 25 Maple Street. | 是枫树公园，就在枫树街25号旁边。
O: Is he awake and breathing? | 他醒着吗？有呼吸吗？
E: Yes. He's awake and talking, but his leg hurts. | 是的。他醒着，还能说话，但是腿疼。
O: Good. An ambulance is on the way. Please stay calm and stay with him. | 好。救护车已经在路上了。请保持冷静，陪在他身边。
E: OK. I'll stay with him until help comes. My friend is here too. | 好的。我会陪着他，直到救援人员来。我的朋友也在这里。
O: Thank you. Tell him help is coming. You can hang up when the paramedics arrive. | 谢谢你。告诉他救援马上就到。医护人员到了以后你就可以挂电话了。
E: The ambulance is here now. Thank you! | 救护车现在到了。谢谢您！
D: 在学校不舒服 | N=Nurse/校医, E=Emma/艾玛

E: I don't feel well. I have a stomachache and I feel dizzy. | 我不舒服。我肚子疼，还头晕。
N: I'm sorry. Why don't you lie down here for a few minutes? | 真难受。你先在这里躺几分钟吧？
E: Thank you. Should I tell my teacher? | 谢谢。我要告诉老师吗？
N: I already called your teacher. Let me take your temperature. | 我已经给你老师打过电话了。我来给你量一下体温。
E: Do I have a fever? | 我发烧了吗？
N: Just a little. Did you eat anything unusual today? Are you allergic to anything? | 有一点点。你今天吃了什么特别的东西吗？你对什么东西过敏吗？
E: I'm not allergic to anything. I just ate a sandwich at lunch. | 我不对什么过敏。我午饭只吃了一个三明治。
N: OK. Can I call your host mom to pick you up? | 好的。我可以给你的寄宿妈妈打电话来接你吗？
E: Yes, please. Can you call my host mom? Her number is in my file. | 好的，麻烦了。你能给我的寄宿妈妈打电话吗？她的号码在我的档案里。
N: Sure. Go home and get some rest. You'll feel better soon. And remember, always tell an adult when you feel sick. | 好的。回家好好休息，你很快就会好起来的。记住，不舒服时一定要告诉大人。
