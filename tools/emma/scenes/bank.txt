D: 开学生账户 | T=Teller/银行柜员, E=Emma/艾玛, K=Karen/凯伦（寄宿妈妈）
T: Good morning. How can I help you today? | 早上好。今天有什么可以帮您？
E: Hi. I'd like to open a bank account, please. Do you have an account for students? | 你好。我想开一个银行账户。你们有学生账户吗？
T: Yes, we do. The student account has no monthly fee. How old are you? | 有的。学生账户没有月费。你多大了？
E: I'm twelve. My host mom Karen is here with me. | 我十二岁。我的寄宿妈妈凯伦陪我来的。
K: Hi. What do we need to bring? | 你好。我们需要带些什么？
T: Her passport and a proof of address. Because she's under eighteen, a parent or guardian needs to sign the form too. | 她的护照和一份地址证明。因为她未满十八岁，家长或监护人也需要在表格上签字。
K: No problem. Here's her passport and a letter with our address. | 没问题。这是她的护照和一封写有我们地址的信。
T: Thank you. Would you also like a savings account? | 谢谢。你还想开一个储蓄账户吗？
E: Yes, please. I want to save money for a new bike. | 好的。我想攒钱买一辆新自行车。
T: Great idea. Your debit card will come in the mail next week. | 好主意。你的借记卡下周会寄过来。
E: Thank you very much! | 非常感谢！
D: 存钱和换钱 | T=Teller/银行柜员, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Hi. I'd like to deposit some money into my account. | 你好。我想往账户里存一些钱。
T: Sure. How much would you like to deposit? | 好的。你想存多少？
E: Fifty dollars. It's my pocket money from this month. | 五十加元。这是我这个月的零花钱。
T: Good for you! Anything else today? | 真棒！今天还需要办别的吗？
E: Can I exchange Chinese yuan for Canadian dollars? | 我可以把人民币换成加元吗？
T: Yes. How much do you have? | 可以。你有多少？
E: Five hundred yuan. What's the exchange rate today? | 五百元人民币。今天的汇率是多少？
T: Today one yuan is about nineteen cents, so you'll get about ninety-five dollars. | 今天一元人民币大约兑十九分，所以你大概能换到九十五加元。
E: OK. Could I have a receipt, please? | 好的。请给我一张收据好吗？
T: Here you go. It also shows your new balance. | 给你。上面也有你的新余额。
E: How can I check my balance later? | 以后我怎么查余额？
T: You can use our app or any of our ATMs. Just remember your PIN. | 你可以用我们的应用程序，或者我们的任何一台取款机。记住你的密码就行。

P: How do I take money out of the ATM? | 怎么从取款机取钱？ | 你第一次用取款机
P: Cover the keypad when you enter your PIN. | 输密码时遮住键盘。 | 大人提醒你用卡安全
P: I'd like to withdraw forty dollars. | 我想取四十美元。 | 你需要一些现金

D: 用取款机取钱 | K=Karen/凯伦（寄宿妈妈）, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Karen, how do I take money out of the ATM? | 凯伦，怎么从取款机取钱？
K: First, insert your card here. Then enter your PIN on the keypad. | 先把卡插在这里，然后在键盘上输入密码。
E: Should I cover the keypad? | 我要遮住键盘吗？
K: Yes. Always cover the keypad when you enter your PIN. | 是的。输密码的时候一定要遮住键盘。
E: OK. I'd like to withdraw forty dollars. | 好的。我想取四十加元。
K: Choose "Withdraw," then type the amount. | 选"取款"，然后输入金额。
E: Is there a fee for using this ATM? | 用这台取款机要手续费吗？
K: Not at your own bank. Other banks may charge you two or three dollars. | 在你自己的银行不用。别的银行可能收两三加元。
E: Here's my money. Should I take the receipt? | 钱出来了。我要拿收据吗？
K: Yes. It helps you keep track of your spending. | 要。它能帮你记录自己的花销。
E: Can I tap my card at the grocery store too? | 我在超市也可以刷卡吗？
K: Yes, you can. You can even pay with your phone. | 可以。你甚至可以用手机付款。
D: 银行卡丢了 | B=Bank worker/银行工作人员, E=Emma/艾玛
B: Hello, Maple Bank. How can I help you? | 您好，枫叶银行。有什么可以帮您？
E: Hi. I think I lost my card. I can't find it anywhere. | 你好。我觉得我的卡丢了，哪里都找不到。
B: I'm sorry to hear that. Don't worry, I can help. Can I have your full name and date of birth? | 很遗憾听到这个消息。别担心，我可以帮你。请告诉我你的全名和出生日期。
E: Emma Wang. My date of birth is May 3rd, 2014. | 王艾玛。我的出生日期是2014年5月3日。
B: Thank you, Emma. Would you like me to freeze your card now? | 谢谢你，艾玛。需要我现在帮你冻结这张卡吗？
E: Yes, please. Can you freeze my card right away? | 好的。请马上帮我冻结我的卡。
B: Done. Nobody can use it now. Do you see any strange payments on your account? | 好了，现在没人能用它了。你的账户上有奇怪的付款吗？
E: Let me check. No, everything looks normal. | 我看一下。没有，一切正常。
B: Good. Please visit your branch with your ID to get a new card. | 很好。请带上你的身份证件到你的支行领一张新卡。
E: Can I use a credit card instead? | 我可以改用信用卡吗？
B: Sorry, you need to be eighteen for a credit card. Your new debit card will be ready in a few days. | 抱歉，满十八岁才能办信用卡。你的新借记卡几天后就能办好。
E: OK. Thank you for your help. | 好的。谢谢你的帮助。

D: 办一张手机卡 | S=Salesperson/店员, E=Emma/艾玛
S: Hi there! What can I do for you? | 你好！有什么可以帮你？
E: Hi. I'd like to get a SIM card, please. I just moved here from China. | 你好。我想办一张手机卡。我刚从中国搬过来。
S: Welcome! Is your phone unlocked? | 欢迎！你的手机是解锁的吗？
E: I think so. My dad checked before I left. | 我想是的。我走之前爸爸检查过了。
S: Great. Do you want prepaid or a monthly plan? | 太好了。你想要预付费还是月套餐？
E: What's the difference between prepaid and a monthly plan? | 预付费和月套餐有什么区别？
S: With prepaid, you pay first and top up when you need more. A monthly plan usually needs a contract. | 预付费就是先付钱，不够了再充值。月套餐通常需要签合同。
E: I think prepaid is better for me. How much data do I get? | 我觉得预付费更适合我。我有多少流量？
S: This one has ten gigs of data and unlimited calls and texts for thirty dollars a month. | 这个套餐每月三十美元，有十G流量，电话和短信不限量。
E: That sounds good. Can you help me set up the SIM card? | 听起来不错。你能帮我装一下手机卡吗？
S: Sure. Here's your new phone number. | 当然。这是你的新电话号码。
D: 流量用完了 | K=Karen/凯伦（寄宿妈妈）, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Karen, I can't open any apps. I think I ran out of data. | 凯伦，我什么应用都打不开。我觉得我的流量用完了。
K: Did you watch a lot of videos this week? | 你这周是不是看了很多视频？
E: Yes, and I had a video call with my parents every day. | 是的，而且我每天都和爸妈视频通话。
K: Try to use the Wi-Fi at home for video calls. It won't use your data. | 在家视频通话尽量用无线网，这样不会用你的流量。
E: Good idea. How do I top up my phone? | 好主意。我怎么给手机充值？
K: You can do it online or with the company's app. | 你可以在网上或者用运营商的应用程序充值。
E: The app is new. Can I use the Wi-Fi to download it? | 那个应用程序我还没有。我可以用无线网下载吗？
K: Of course. Also, we're going on a camping trip next weekend. | 当然。还有，我们下周末要去露营。
E: Will my phone work there? | 我的手机在那里能用吗？
K: The signal is weak in the mountains, so download your music before we go. | 山里信号很弱，所以出发前把音乐下载好。
E: OK. Will they charge me extra for that? | 好的。下载会额外收费吗？
K: Not if you use the Wi-Fi at home. | 如果用家里的无线网就不会。
