## booking | 订位与入座

W: reservation | 预订
W: table for two | 两人桌
W: under the name | 以……的名字
W: fully booked | 订满了
W: wait | 等待
W: waiting list | 等候名单
W: inside | 室内
W: outside | 室外
W: menu | 菜单
W: host | 领位员
W: waiter | 服务员（男）
W: waitress | 服务员（女）
W: high chair | 儿童高脚椅
W: busy | 忙碌的
W: quiet | 安静的
W: corner | 角落

W: ready to order | 准备好点餐
W: special | 特色菜
P: I'd like to book a table for four at seven o'clock. | 我想订一张七点钟的四人桌。 | 你打电话给餐厅订位
P: We have a reservation under the name Zhang. | 我们以"张"的名字订了位。 | 你到了餐厅，告诉服务员你订过位
P: A table for three, please. | 请给我们一张三人桌。 | 你们三个人没订位就进了餐厅
P: How long is the wait? | 要等多久？ | 餐厅很忙，你想知道要等多久
P: Could we sit by the window? | 我们可以坐在窗边吗？ | 你想坐靠窗的位置
P: Could we have the menu, please? | 请给我们菜单好吗？ | 你坐下后想看菜单
P: We need a few more minutes. | 我们还需要几分钟。 | 服务员来点餐，但你还没决定
D: 打电话订位 | H=Host/餐厅接待, E=Emma/艾玛
H: Good afternoon, Luigi's Restaurant. How can I help you? | 下午好，路易吉餐厅。有什么可以帮您？
E: Hi, I'd like to book a table for Saturday evening, please. | 你好，我想订周六晚上的位子。
H: Sure. For how many people? | 好的。几位？
E: Four people. At seven o'clock, if possible. | 四位。如果可以的话，七点钟。
H: Let me check. ... I'm sorry, seven is fully booked. We have a table at seven thirty. | 我查一下。……抱歉，七点已经订满了。七点半有一张桌子。
E: Seven thirty is fine. | 七点半可以。
H: Great. What name is it under? | 好的。用什么名字预订？
E: Zhang. Z-H-A-N-G. | 张。Z-H-A-N-G。
H: And a phone number, please? | 请留个电话号码？
E: It's 07700 900123. Could we sit by the window? | 07700 900123。我们可以坐在窗边吗？
H: I'll make a note. See you on Saturday! | 我记下了。周六见！
D: 到店入座 | S=Waiter/服务员, E=Emma/艾玛
S: Good evening. Do you have a reservation? | 晚上好。您有预订吗？
E: Yes, we have a reservation under the name Zhang, for seven thirty. | 有，我们用"张"的名字订了七点半。
S: Let me see. ... Yes, a table for four. This way, please. | 我看看。……有，四人桌。这边请。
E: Thank you. Is this table by the window? | 谢谢。这张桌子靠窗吗？
S: Yes, it is. Here are your menus. Today's special is mushroom soup. | 是的。这是你们的菜单。今天的特色菜是蘑菇汤。
E: That sounds nice. Could we have some water first? | 听起来不错。能先给我们一些水吗？
S: Of course. Still or sparkling? | 当然。普通水还是气泡水？
E: Still, please. | 普通水，谢谢。
S: I'll be right back. Are you ready to order, or do you need a few minutes? | 我马上回来。你们准备好点餐了吗，还是需要几分钟？
E: We need a few more minutes, please. | 我们还需要几分钟，谢谢。

P: I'll have the chicken salad, please. | 我要鸡肉沙拉，谢谢。 | 你决定点鸡肉沙拉
P: What do you recommend? | 你推荐什么？ | 你不知道点什么好
P: I'm allergic to nuts. Does this have nuts in it? | 我对坚果过敏，这个里面有坚果吗？ | 你对坚果过敏，想确认菜里有没有
P: Is this dish spicy? | 这道菜辣吗？ | 你不太能吃辣
P: Could I have it without onions? | 我可以不要洋葱吗？ | 你不喜欢洋葱
P: Could I have the sauce on the side? | 酱汁可以另外放吗？ | 你想自己决定放多少酱汁
P: Do you have any vegetarian dishes? | 你们有素菜吗？ | 你的朋友吃素
P: Can we share a dessert? | 我们可以分一份甜点吗？ | 你们吃得很饱，只想点一份甜点
D: 点主菜 | S=Waitress/服务员, E=Emma/艾玛
S: Are you ready to order? | 可以点餐了吗？
E: Yes. What do you recommend? | 可以。你推荐什么？
S: The grilled salmon is very popular, and the pasta is homemade. | 烤三文鱼很受欢迎，意面是店里自己做的。
E: Is the pasta spicy? | 意面辣吗？
S: It's a little spicy, but not too much. | 有一点辣，但不太辣。
E: I'll have the grilled salmon, then. But I'm allergic to nuts. Is that OK? | 那我要烤三文鱼。不过我对坚果过敏，没问题吧？
S: Good question. The salmon comes with a nut sauce, but we can make it without. | 问得好。三文鱼配的是坚果酱，但我们可以不放。
E: Yes, please make it without nuts. | 好的，请不要放坚果。
S: No problem. Would you like a side dish? | 没问题。要配菜吗？
E: A green salad, please, with the dressing on the side. | 一份蔬菜沙拉，谢谢，沙拉酱另外放。
S: Sure. Anything to drink? | 好的。喝点什么？
E: An orange juice, please. | 一杯橙汁，谢谢。
D: 点甜点 | S=Waiter/服务员, E=Emma/艾玛
S: How was everything? | 一切都还好吗？
E: It was delicious, thank you. The salmon was great. | 很好吃，谢谢。三文鱼很棒。
S: I'm glad you liked it. Would you like to see the dessert menu? | 很高兴您喜欢。要看看甜点菜单吗？
E: Yes, please. ... What's the chocolate cake like? | 好的。……巧克力蛋糕怎么样？
S: It's warm and served with vanilla ice cream. It's our most popular dessert. | 是热的，配香草冰淇淋。这是我们最受欢迎的甜点。
E: It sounds great, but I'm quite full. Can we share one? | 听起来很棒，但我挺饱的。我们可以分一份吗？
S: Of course. I'll bring two spoons. | 当然。我拿两把勺子来。
E: Perfect. Could we also have some more water? | 太好了。能再给我们一些水吗？
S: Sure, I'll bring it right away. | 好的，我马上拿来。

D: 结账AA | S=Waiter/服务员, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Excuse me, could we have the bill, please? | 打扰一下，请给我们结账好吗？
S: Of course. Here you are. | 好的，给您。
E: Could we split the bill? There are two of us. | 我们可以分开付吗？我们两个人。
S: Sure. That's twelve pounds fifty each. | 当然。每人十二英镑五十。
E: Is service included? | 服务费包含在内了吗？
S: No, it isn't. A tip is up to you. | 不包含，小费随您。
E: OK. Can I pay by card? | 好的。我可以刷卡吗？
S: Yes. You can tap your card here. It's contactless. | 可以。把卡在这里碰一下就行，非接触式的。
E: Great. Could I have a receipt, please? | 太好了。可以给我一张收据吗？
S: Here's your receipt. Thank you, and have a nice evening! | 这是您的收据。谢谢，祝您晚上愉快！
D: 上错菜了 | S=Waitress/服务员, E=Emma/艾玛
S: Here's your burger. Enjoy! | 这是您的汉堡。请慢用！
E: Sorry, this isn't what I ordered. I ordered the chicken wrap. | 抱歉，这不是我点的。我点的是鸡肉卷。
S: Oh, I'm so sorry about that. I'll change it right away. | 哦，真是抱歉。我马上给您换。
E: Thank you. No problem. | 谢谢，没关系。
S: Here's your chicken wrap. Sorry again for the mistake. | 这是您的鸡肉卷。再次为出错道歉。
E: That's OK. It looks great. | 没事，看起来很棒。
S: The drinks are free today because of the mix-up. | 因为弄错了，今天的饮料免费。
E: Oh, that's very kind of you. Thank you! | 哦，你们太好了，谢谢！
S: You're welcome. Just let me know if you need anything else. | 不客气。需要什么再告诉我。
E: Actually, could I have a box for the leftovers later? | 对了，一会儿可以给我一个盒子装剩菜吗？
S: Of course. Just ask when you're finished. | 当然，吃完了说一声就行。
