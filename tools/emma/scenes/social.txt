D: 第一次见面 | M=Mia/米娅, E=Emma/艾玛
M: Hi! Are you the new exchange student? | 嗨！你是新来的交换生吗？
E: Yes, I am. I'm Emma. I'm from Shanghai. | 是的，我是艾玛，来自上海。
M: Nice to meet you, Emma. I'm Mia. | 很高兴认识你，艾玛。我是米娅。
E: Nice to meet you too. Are you in Year Seven as well? | 我也很高兴认识你。你也是七年级吗？
M: Yes, we're in the same class! What do you like doing in your free time? | 是的，我们同班！你空闲时喜欢做什么？
E: I'm really into drawing, especially cartoons. What about you? | 我特别喜欢画画，尤其是漫画。你呢？
M: No way! I love drawing too. I'm in the art club. | 不会吧！我也喜欢画画。我在美术社团。
E: We have a lot in common! Can I join the art club? | 我们有很多共同点！我可以加入美术社团吗？
M: Of course. We meet every Wednesday after school. Come with me! | 当然。我们每周三放学后活动，跟我一起去吧！
E: That would be great. Thanks, Mia! | 那太好了，谢谢你，米娅！
D: 午餐时聊天 | T=Tom/汤姆, E=Emma/艾玛
T: Hey, can I sit here? | 嘿，我可以坐这儿吗？
E: Sure, go ahead. | 当然，请坐。
T: Thanks. I'm Tom. You're new, right? | 谢谢。我是汤姆。你是新来的，对吧？
E: Yes, I'm Emma. I started last week. | 是的，我是艾玛，上周开始上学的。
T: How do you like it here so far? | 到目前为止你觉得这里怎么样？
E: I like it a lot. Everyone is friendly, but the food is very different. | 我很喜欢。大家都很友好，不过饭菜很不一样。
T: Ha! What's your favourite food back home? | 哈！你在家乡最喜欢吃什么？
E: Dumplings, definitely. My grandma makes the best ones. | 肯定是饺子。我奶奶包的饺子最好吃。
T: I've never tried real Chinese dumplings. | 我从来没吃过正宗的中国饺子。
E: Maybe we can make some together one day! | 也许哪天我们可以一起包饺子！
T: I'd love that. | 我很乐意。

D: 约看电影 | M=Mia/米娅, E=Emma/艾玛
M: Emma, are you free on Saturday? | 艾玛，你周六有空吗？
E: I think so. Why? | 应该有空。怎么了？
M: A few of us are going to the cinema to see the new Pixar film. Do you want to come? | 我们几个人要去电影院看皮克斯的新片。你想一起来吗？
E: I'd love to! I need to ask my host parents first, though. | 我很想去！不过我得先问问我的寄宿父母。
M: Sure. The film starts at two thirty. | 好的。电影两点半开始。
E: Where shall we meet? | 我们在哪里见？
M: Let's meet at the bus stop outside school at two. | 我们两点在学校外面的公交车站见吧。
E: Sounds good. I have to be back by six, is that OK? | 听起来不错。我必须六点之前回来，可以吗？
M: No problem. It finishes at about four thirty. | 没问题，大概四点半就结束了。
E: Great. I'll text you tonight. | 太好了，我今晚给你发消息。
D: 改约 | T=Tom/汤姆, E=Emma/艾玛
T: Hi Emma, do you want to hang out after school today? We're playing football in the park. | 嗨艾玛，你今天放学后想一起玩吗？我们在公园踢足球。
E: Sorry, I can't make it today. I have a piano lesson. | 抱歉，我今天去不了，我有钢琴课。
T: Oh, that's OK. How about tomorrow? | 哦，没关系。明天怎么样？
E: Tomorrow I'm free. What time? | 明天我有空。几点？
T: At four. We usually play for an hour. | 四点，我们一般玩一个小时。
E: Perfect. But I'm not very good at football. | 好极了。不过我足球踢得不太好。
T: Don't worry. We just play for fun. | 别担心，我们只是玩着开心。
E: OK, count me in! | 好的，算我一个！
T: Great! See you tomorrow at the park gate. | 太好了！明天公园门口见。
E: See you! | 再见！

D: 陌生人加好友 | K=Kate/凯特（寄宿家庭姐姐）, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Kate, a stranger just sent me a message on Instagram. | 凯特，刚才有个陌生人在Instagram上给我发消息。
K: What did they say? | 他们说什么了？
E: They asked which school I go to and where I live. | 他们问我在哪个学校上学，住在哪里。
K: Don't reply. You should never share personal information with strangers. | 别回复。你永远不要和陌生人分享个人信息。
E: Should I block them? | 我应该把他们拉黑吗？
K: Yes. You can block and report them. Let me show you how. | 应该。你可以拉黑并举报他们。我教你怎么做。
E: Thanks. Should I make my profile private too? | 谢谢。我也应该把个人资料设为私密吗？

E: OK, done. I feel much safer now. | 好了，设置好了。我现在觉得安全多了。
K: Good. And always tell an adult if something feels wrong. | 很好。如果觉得有什么不对劲，一定要告诉大人。
