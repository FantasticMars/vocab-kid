D: 见到寄宿家庭 | K=Karen/凯伦（寄宿妈妈）, E=Emma/艾玛
K: Hi, you must be Emma! Welcome to Canada. I'm Karen, your host mom. | 嗨，你一定是艾玛！欢迎来到加拿大。我是凯伦，你的寄宿妈妈。
E: Hi, Karen. Nice to meet you. Thank you for having me. | 嗨，凯伦，很高兴见到你。谢谢你们接待我。
K: How was your flight? You must be tired. | 飞行怎么样？你一定累了吧。
E: It was long, but I'm OK. I slept a little on the plane. | 很长，但我还好。我在飞机上睡了一会儿。
K: Let me help you with your suitcase. The car is just outside. | 我来帮你拿行李箱。车就在外面。
E: Thanks. Is it far to your house? | 谢谢。到你家远吗？
K: About twenty minutes. You'll meet my husband, Dave, and our son, Jack. | 大约二十分钟。你会见到我丈夫戴夫和我们的儿子杰克。
E: How old is Jack? | 杰克几岁了？
K: He's ten. He's really excited to meet you. We also have a dog called Max. | 他十岁。他特别期待见到你。我们还有一只叫麦克斯的狗。
E: I love dogs! I brought some gifts from China for your family. | 我喜欢狗！我从中国给你们家带了些礼物。
K: Oh, that's so sweet of you. Let's go home. | 哦，你真贴心。我们回家吧。
D: 参观新家 | K=Karen/凯伦（寄宿妈妈）, E=Emma/艾玛
K: This is your bedroom. I hope you like it. | 这是你的卧室，希望你喜欢。
E: It's lovely! Where can I put my clothes? | 太好了！我的衣服放在哪里？
K: There's a closet here and some drawers under the bed. | 这里有一个衣柜，床下还有几个抽屉。
E: Great. Where is the bathroom? | 好的。卫生间在哪里？
K: It's across the hall. You'll share it with Jack. | 在走廊对面。你和杰克共用。
E: OK. What's the Wi-Fi password, please? | 好的。请问Wi-Fi密码是多少？
K: It's on the fridge. Help yourself to snacks in the kitchen anytime. | 在冰箱上。厨房里的零食你随时可以吃。
E: Thank you. I'd like to call my parents to tell them I arrived safely. | 谢谢。我想给爸妈打个电话，告诉他们我平安到了。
K: Of course. Take your time and unpack. Dinner is at six. | 当然。你慢慢整理行李。六点吃晚饭。
E: Do you need any help with dinner? | 晚饭需要我帮忙吗？
K: That's kind of you. You can set the table later. | 你真好。你一会儿可以摆餐具。

P: What can I do to help around the house? | 我能帮家里做点什么？ | 你想主动帮寄宿家庭做家务
P: Should I take off my shoes in the house? | 在家里我需要脱鞋吗？ | 你不确定进屋要不要脱鞋

P: Thanks for explaining everything. | 谢谢你把一切都讲清楚。 | 寄宿爸爸给你讲完了家规
D: 了解家规 | K=Karen/凯伦（寄宿妈妈）, E=Emma/艾玛
K: Emma, can we talk about a few house rules? | 艾玛，我们聊聊几条家规好吗？
E: Sure. I want to do things the right way. | 当然。我想把事情做对。
K: On school nights, we like everyone to be home by six for dinner. | 上学的晚上，我们希望大家六点前回家吃晚饭。
E: OK. What if I have an after-school club? | 好的。如果我有课后社团怎么办？
K: Just text me and let me know. That's fine. | 发短信告诉我一声就行，没关系。
E: Got it. Is there a curfew on weekends? | 明白了。周末有门禁时间吗？
K: Nine o'clock, and please tell us where you're going. | 九点，而且请告诉我们你去哪里。
E: No problem. Can I do my own laundry? | 没问题。我可以自己洗衣服吗？
K: Yes. The washing machine is in the basement. I'll show you how to use it. | 可以。洗衣机在地下室，我教你怎么用。
E: Thank you. And should I take off my shoes in the house? | 谢谢。在家里我需要脱鞋吗？
K: Yes, please. Just leave them by the front door. | 是的，请把鞋放在前门旁边。
D: 帮忙做家务 | V=Dave/戴夫（寄宿爸爸）, E=Emma/艾玛
V: Morning, Emma! Did you sleep well? | 早上好，艾玛！睡得好吗？
E: Yes, thanks. What can I do to help around the house? | 睡得好，谢谢。我能帮家里做点什么？
V: Everyone has chores here. Could you take out the recycling on Tuesdays? | 这里每个人都有家务。你能每周二把可回收垃圾拿出去吗？
E: Sure. Where does the garbage go? | 当然。普通垃圾放哪里？
V: The garbage goes in the black bin, and the recycling goes in the blue bin. | 普通垃圾放黑色垃圾桶，可回收的放蓝色垃圾桶。
E: OK. Should I make my bed every morning? | 好的。我需要每天早上整理床铺吗？
V: That would be great. And please keep your room tidy. | 那就太好了。也请保持房间整洁。
E: I will. Can I use the microwave for lunch? | 我会的。午饭我可以用微波炉吗？
V: Of course. Just don't put anything metal in it. | 当然。只是别把金属东西放进去。
E: Thanks for explaining everything. | 谢谢你把一切都讲清楚。
V: No problem. Let us know if you need anything. | 不客气。需要什么就告诉我们。

D: 认识室友 | S=Sophie/索菲（室友）, E=Emma/艾玛
S: Hi! Are you my new roommate? I'm Sophie. | 嗨！你是我的新室友吗？我是索菲。
E: Yes, I'm Emma. Nice to meet you! Which bed is mine? | 是的，我是艾玛，很高兴认识你！哪张床是我的？
S: The one by the window. I hope that's OK. | 靠窗的那张，希望你不介意。
E: That's perfect. I like the view. | 太好了，我喜欢这个景色。
S: We share the desk, so let's keep it neat. | 我们共用书桌，所以要保持整齐哦。
E: Sure. Do you mind if I put a photo on the wall? | 当然。你介意我在墙上贴张照片吗？
S: Not at all. Is that your family? | 一点也不介意。那是你的家人吗？
E: Yes, that's my mom, my dad and my little brother. | 是的，这是我妈妈、爸爸和弟弟。
S: Cute! By the way, lights out is at ten on weeknights. | 真可爱！顺便说一下，工作日晚上十点熄灯。
E: OK. I usually go to bed early anyway. | 好的。反正我一般睡得早。
S: Great. I think we'll get along well. | 太好了，我觉得我们会相处得很好。
D: 找宿管老师帮忙 | L=Ms. Lee/李老师（宿管）, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Excuse me, Ms. Lee. Do you have a minute? | 打扰一下，李老师。您有空吗？
L: Of course, Emma. What's up? | 当然，艾玛。怎么了？
E: I think I lost my room key. I can't find it anywhere. | 我好像把房间钥匙弄丢了，到处都找不到。
L: Don't worry. I have a spare key. Let me open the door for you. | 别担心，我有备用钥匙。我来帮你开门。
E: Thank you so much. Also, the heater in our room isn't working. | 太感谢了。还有，我们房间的暖气坏了。
L: I'll report it to maintenance today. They'll fix it tomorrow. | 我今天就报修，他们明天会修好。
E: And where is the laundry room? | 洗衣房在哪里？
L: It's on the first floor, next to the common room. | 在一楼，公共休息室旁边。
E: Can I use the kitchen in the evening? | 晚上我可以用厨房吗？
L: Yes, until nine thirty. Please clean up after yourself. | 可以，到九点半为止。用完请自己收拾干净。
E: Got it. Thanks for your help, Ms. Lee. | 明白了。谢谢您的帮助，李老师。
