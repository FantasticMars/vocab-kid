D: 参加足球选拔 | C=Coach Taylor/泰勒教练, E=Emma/艾玛
E: Excuse me, Coach Taylor. How can I join the team? | 打扰一下，泰勒教练。我怎么才能加入球队？
C: Hi! Are you interested in soccer? We have tryouts next Monday after school. | 你好！你对足球感兴趣吗？我们下周一放学后有选拔赛。
E: Yes! I played soccer in China for two years. | 是的！我在中国踢了两年足球。
C: Great. What position do you play? | 太好了。你打什么位置？
E: I usually play defense, but I can be goalie too. | 我通常打防守，但我也能当守门员。
C: Wonderful. For tryouts, please bring shin guards, cleats, and a water bottle. | 太好了。参加选拔请带上护腿板、足球鞋和一个水壶。
E: What should I bring to practice if I make the team? | 如果我入选了，训练要带什么？
C: The same things. Practice is on Tuesdays and Thursdays. We always warm up for ten minutes first. | 一样的东西。每周二和周四训练。我们总是先热身十分钟。
E: OK. I'll be there on Monday. | 好的，我周一会去的。
C: See you then, Emma. Don't be nervous. Just have fun! | 到时见，艾玛。别紧张，玩得开心就好！
D: 比赛之后 | A=Ava/艾娃（队友）, E=Emma/艾玛
A: Good game, Emma! That was a nice shot in the second half. | 打得好，艾玛！你下半场那一脚射得真好。
E: Thanks, Ava! But I didn't score a goal. | 谢谢，艾娃！但我没进球。
A: It was so close! You kicked the ball really hard. | 就差一点！你踢得很有力。
E: You passed the ball to me at the perfect time. You're a great teammate. | 你传球的时机太好了。你是个很棒的队友。
A: Thanks! Too bad we didn't win. | 谢谢！可惜我们没赢。
E: At least we didn't lose. A tie is not bad. | 至少我们没输。平局也不错。
A: True. We'll win next time. | 没错。我们下次会赢的。
E: Do you want to practice kicking together this weekend? | 这周末你想一起练习射门吗？
A: Sure! Let's meet at the park on Saturday. | 好啊！我们周六在公园见吧。
E: Sounds good. See you then! | 好的，到时见！
