## Attribution

Example sentences marked as coming from Tatoeba are from the Tatoeba corpus (https://tatoeba.org), licensed CC BY 2.0 FR.
IPA derived from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (Carnegie Mellon University).
Chinese glosses partly from ECDICT (MIT, © skywind3000).

## Known limitations

- The 二级 (primary) list was reconstructed from incomplete OCR (484 of about 505 words). As a result, some very basic words (for example to, as, if, time) ended up in L2.
- Collocations are shown only when they are frequent in Tatoeba. Coverage: L1 414/558, L2 885/1134, KET 1093/1688, PET 741/1473.
- Tatoeba sentences are community-written. Filters and hand overrides were applied, but a few examples may still sound unusual.
- KyleBing glosses have no explicit license. Short dictionary glosses are largely factual, but if this matters they can be swapped for ECDICT and override glosses by re-running `build_levels.py` with KyleBing removed.
